@ RESEARCH::fork-context-no-parent-runtime  •  —  •  #decision #fork #skills #lifecycle
With Claude Code's `context: fork` skill frontmatter, the entire skill body becomes the forked subagent's prompt; there is no separate parent runtime that resumes after the subagent finishes. Lifecycle steps that were originally documented as "skill body owns this after the curator returns" must be performed by the subagent itself, or they will be silently skipped. Symptom seen 2026-04-30: wiki-digest's Step 6a (`_session.md` reset) phrased as "the skill body owns the LIFECYCLE around the curator's work" caused the curator to write "will be reset by the skill body" in its summary and never perform the Write — leaving a fully-populated inbox after a successful digest. Fix pattern: in fork-mode skill bodies, address every step in second person ("you, the curator, perform…") and explicitly forbid deferring to "the skill body" or "the calling runtime". Where the agent's tool list is narrower than the skill body's allowed-tools (e.g. curator has Write/Edit but no Bash), use Write-based fallbacks (tombstone markers instead of `rm`) rather than letting the gap silently drop the step.
