@ ARCHITECTURE::piped-wiki-link-contract  •  wiki/Rules.md  •  #wiki #linking #obsidian #conventions
Rules.md §3 and §7 mandate the piped form `[[note-basename|Display Title]]` for every internal wiki-link; bare `[[Title]]` is explicitly forbidden. The basename is the target file's name without `.md` (kebab-case per §5); the part after `|` is the human-readable display. Reason: Obsidian's default resolver matches against filenames, not H1 titles, so a bare `[[Recall Path]]` does not resolve to `recall-path.md` and clicking it creates a blank note at the vault root.
